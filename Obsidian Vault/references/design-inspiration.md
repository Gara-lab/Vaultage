# Design inspiration references

External sites worth checking during a design-canvas intake, before inventing a layout
from scratch. Both are human-browsing inspiration libraries, not tools or APIs — nothing
to install, nothing to download, just links.

- **UXArchive** — https://www.uxarchive.app/ — the largest library of real mobile-app
  user flows (onboarding, checkout, empty states, date pickers…), organized by app and
  by flow type.
- **Patttterns** — https://patttterns.com/ — ~400 curated real UI pattern screenshots
  (forms, success states, navigation, email layouts), by product makers for product
  makers.
